A one-sentence satirical roast for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-swift-raven-16-the-long-roast-lawmakers-and-the-f3a3bdf7): a senator who wrote the law requiring every government document to be a PDF, filing his own AI-safety bill as a crooked phone photo of his laptop screen.

## Run

The grader runs `node main.ts` (Node 22). It writes one sentence to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/output/entry.txt`), validates 1–320 characters, one sentence, and a byte-identical round-trip.
